HOW TO GET THE OPENVC INVESTOR DATABASE (16,000-20,000 verified profiles)
=========================================================================

The OpenVC investor database at openvc.app cannot be bulk-downloaded
programmatically -- the site blocks automated requests (403) and requires
a logged-in account for CSV export. Here is how to get it manually:

OPTION 1: OpenVC CRM Export (RECOMMENDED - most complete data)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://www.openvc.app and create a free account
2. Go to https://www.openvc.app/search
3. Use filters to select investors (sector, stage, geography, check size)
4. Add investors to your CRM shortlist
5. Go to CRM -> click "More" -> Export as CSV or XLS
NOTE: Export excludes investor emails and LinkedIn for privacy reasons.
NOTE: You may need to do this in batches by filtering different categories.

OPTION 2: OpenVC Curated Lists (free CSV downloads by category)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/
2. Browse lists organized by industry, stage, and geography, e.g.:
   - https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/entertainment-investors
   - https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/community-investors
   - https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/collaboration-tools-investors
3. Each list page should have a CSV download option
4. Download individual lists and merge them

OPTION 3: findfunding.vc (open source alternative, US/Canada focus)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://www.findfunding.vc/list-details
2. Data is backed by Airtable (base ID: appOwFUZ6NgFbP0jJ)
3. Pre-Seed to Series A VC firms in US and Canada
4. No direct bulk export -- browse on the website

WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE (this directory)
--------------------------------------
File: openvc-investors.csv
- 30,893 unique investors (17,652 firms + 13,080 individuals + 161 supplementary)
- Source: CrunchBase 2015 open data export + DoltHub openbook + connor11528 GitHub
- Columns: investor_name, investor_permalink, country_code, state_code,
           region, city, investment_count, funding_round_types, num_portfolio_companies
- Sorted by investment_count (most active investors first)
- Covers 80+ countries, top: USA (8,401), GBR (1,032), CAN (466), DEU (421)
- Investment stages: venture, seed, angel, private_equity, debt_financing, etc.

CAVEAT: This data is from the 2015 CrunchBase open export. For current,
verified data with sectors/check sizes, use OpenVC directly (Option 1 or 2).
